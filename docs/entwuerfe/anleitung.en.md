# How to play Roast & Rise

> Draft, 2026-09-28, matching the game after step 5. "Roast & Rise" is a working title. The numbers come from `themes/kaffeeroesterei/theme.json`; a test checks that they match the game.

You run a small coffee roastery and grow it into a coffee empire. Your currency is **beans**.

## Roasting

Tap or click the big bean to roast beans. Every click earns beans, one per click at the start. That is how you pay for your first equipment.

## Equipment

In **Equipment** you buy tools and businesses that make beans on their own. It starts with the roasting pan and goes on through drum roasters, coffee carts and cafés to the cargo ship.

- Every purchase raises the price of the next one.
- **×1**, **×10** and **Max** set how many you buy at once.
- New equipment appears once you own the one before it.

## Upgrades

Upgrades double the output of one kind of equipment, raise all income or make your clicks stronger. They appear once you own or have earned enough.

## Achievements

Special milestones earn you achievements, such as your first roasting pan or your first million beans. **Achievements** shows what is still missing.

## Reputation (prestige)

Once a roastery has earned 50,000 beans, you can **sell it and start over**.

- Beans, equipment and upgrades start again from zero.
- In return you gain **reputation**, and every point of reputation permanently raises all income by 10%.
- The more you earn before selling, the more reputation you get.

## While you are away

Your roastery keeps working while the game is closed. When you come back, you get 50% of the usual output for the time you were away, for up to 8 hours.

## Double income

Below the bean you can unlock double income for 10 minutes, either by choosing to watch an ad or by paying with beans. You can also double your earnings from time away by watching an ad. Otherwise, ads only appear in short breaks after you sell a roastery.

> Include the ads part only if the site shows ads.

## Saving and language

- The game saves automatically in your browser. Depending on the browser, private windows can't save; the game tells you if so.
- The gear at the top lets you switch the language (English or German).
- There you can also reset your progress.

## Tips

- Buy several roasting pans early; they pay for themselves quickly.
- Keep an eye on upgrades; they often bring more than another machine.
- Sell your first roastery once reputation clearly boosts your income, for example when it offers 10 points.
