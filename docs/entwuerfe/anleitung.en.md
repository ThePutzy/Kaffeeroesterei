# How to play Roast & Rise

> Draft, 2026-09-28, matching the game after step 5. "Roast & Rise" is a working title. The numbers come from `themes/kaffeeroesterei/theme.json`; a test checks the most important ones against the game (reputation threshold and bonus, earnings while away, boost duration).

You run a small coffee roastery and grow it into a coffee empire. Your currency is **beans**.

## Roasting

Tap or click the big bean to roast beans. Each click earns beans, one per click at first. That is how you pay for your first equipment.

## Equipment

In the **Equipment** tab you buy tools and businesses that make beans on their own. You start with the roasting pan and work your way up through drum roasters, coffee carts and cafés to the cargo ship.

- Every purchase raises the price of the next one.
- **×1**, **×10** and **Max** set how many you buy at once.
- New equipment appears once you own the one before it.

## Upgrades

Upgrades double the output of one kind of equipment, raise your total production or make your clicks stronger. They appear once you own enough equipment, have roasted often enough or have earned enough in this roastery.

## Achievements

Special milestones earn you achievements, such as your first roasting pan or your first million beans. The **Achievements** tab shows which ones you still need.

## Reputation (prestige)

Once a roastery has earned 50,000 beans, you can **sell it and start over**.

- Beans, equipment and upgrades are reset.
- In return you gain **reputation**, and every point of reputation permanently raises all income by 10%.
- The more you earn before selling, the more reputation you get.

## While you are away

Your roastery keeps working while the game is closed or in the background. If you come back after more than a minute, you get 50% of the usual output for the time you were away, for up to 8 hours.

## Double income

Below the bean you can buy double income for 10 minutes, paid with beans.

> Include the following sentences only if the site shows ads:

Instead of paying with beans, you can also choose to watch an ad. You can double your earnings from time away by watching an ad, too. Apart from that, ads only appear in short breaks after you sell a roastery.

## Saving and language

- The game saves automatically in your browser.
- In a private window, your progress is lost when you close the window. Some browsers can't save there at all; if so, the game will let you know.
- The gear at the top lets you switch the language (English or German).
- There you can also reset your progress.

## Tips

- Buy several roasting pans early; they pay for themselves quickly.
- Keep an eye on upgrades; they are often worth more than another machine.
- Sell your first roastery once reputation clearly boosts your income, for example once a sale would give you 10 points.
